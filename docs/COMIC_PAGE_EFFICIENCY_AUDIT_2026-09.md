### Additional root gaps the new route must not preserve

- **The first comic panel is not drawn.** `mc_page_render.py:267–275` paints it fully into the preroll; `:381` explicitly bypasses its reveal. This supplies an immediate story image but does not meet the owner's all-content hand-drawn request. The replacement must preserve a quick story hook while visibly drawing the opening too, rather than reverting to a long empty template.
- **An incomplete comic can pass the current art floor.** `motionComic.ts:1663` only rejects coverage below 90%, with panel 0 mandatory. For 10–12 panels, one missing later panel can survive; `:1792` removes missing-art/audio panels from the render timeline. Page admission needs exact approved story-beat coverage, not a percentage floor. This is a source-demonstrated branch, not a claim that a particular live run lost a panel.
- **Current reveal timing is not page-aware.** `mc_page_render.py:30` uses MOVE/HOLD 0.55 seconds and DRAW_FRAC 0.62. Each nonopening panel's draw span is `max(0.45, (duration − 1.1) × 0.62)`. It does not adapt pace to page-region detail, reading load, or a complete-page coverage contract.
- **Page pixels are a quality risk.** A 1920×1088 image split into six cells cannot be assumed equivalent to six independent 1920×1088 inputs viewed in camera closeups. The existing generic atlas minimum of 512 pixels is not proof of comic face, linework, or bubble readability at final framing. Do not claim savings by silently shrinking the art or reusing the unchanged 2K atlas setting.

### Reference-capable code is not automatically an approved comic route

There is an existing real image-conditioning adapter: `src/lib/falImage.ts:310` folds reference images into an actual `image_url` sent to Fal FLUX Kontext (`fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext`, selected in `src/lib/falImagePricing.ts:116`). This establishes a transport capability in source, **not** current provider availability, an accepted comic-quality benchmark, or authority to replace the pinned comic worker.

It also cannot be adopted unchanged for the requested strict character continuity: `foldReferencesToSheet` catches composition errors and silently returns only the first reference; the contact-sheet planner caps at nine; and `imageSize` is documented as a no-op. The sheet helper's digest uses IDs/angles, not underlying image bytes. These need explicit full-reference coverage, content-bound provenance, geometry checks, and fail-closed behavior in any reuse. Do not present a fallback-to-one-reference render as if it consumed all the approved angles.

The production `visual_matter_references` contract (`src/engine/moduleContracts.ts:1069`) explicitly describes its Z-Image reference assets as **QA-only**, not image-to-image inputs. `buildMotionComicCharacterArtRequest` and the optional `refs` prompt helper are not called by the active comic generation loop. No verified Ernie reference-conditioned comic worker was established by this audit. The missing requirement is therefore a qualified, pinned, sufficiently high-resolution image-conditioning route carrying the actual immutable character references into generation—not a prompt phrase, seed, LoRA catalog record, or QA-only comparison asset. Verify the intended approved Ernie worker's real capability before choosing/implementing that transport; if it cannot satisfy it, surface that consequential provider decision rather than silently adopting Fal or reducing quality.

## Recommended high-impact root change

Make a **reviewed page artifact** the shared production unit between story planning, image generation, reveal, recovery, and assembly. Do not bolt an atlas image onto the existing per-panel paid loop.

1. The automated planner produces a typed page plan from the accepted story: stable page/panel IDs, ordered story-beat IDs, channel/style identity, participating character IDs, actual source-region geometry, narration cues, protected faces/lettering space, and required visual details. Permit genuinely different page layouts; the current fixed-grid atlas alone is not the requested layout freedom.
2. Acquire or reuse approved multiview character assets once. Bind their **content hashes, actual bytes, character identities, and revisions** to every relevant page request and to its provider receipt. Use a genuinely reference-capable approved renderer/worker with a verified contract. Current Z-Image text-only compatibility must not masquerade as Ernie or as reference conditioning. Provider availability/capability needs its own verified pilot before selecting a replacement route.
3. Generate **one complete art image per page** at a measured, sufficient native canvas size. Derive/recover panel regions from that actual generated page and independently verify count, order, boundaries, people, props, channel style, and usable negative space. A requested box or model-produced JSON is not proof the pixels obey it. Do not reconstruct the supposed candidate from separately generated panel images.
4. Feed that exact page image and reviewed region/reveal manifest into the deterministic renderer. The page raster is the source, **not a fully visible background**: unrevealed story regions remain masked until their ordered narration-aligned hand trace. Keep all previous revealed regions and reveal every panel—including the first—with the hand. Preserve native, editable narration-timed lettering and safe face placement. No image-model lettering migration is necessary for this efficiency work; the protected thumbnail module is unrelated.
5. Checkpoint accepted source pages, references, crops/regions, audio, and reviews independently. A layout-only repair consumes the existing page; a failed page alone may regenerate under a bounded, receipt-aware retry. Never rerun accepted pages or regenerate references after an assembly failure.

Call-count hypothesis, **not measured improvement**: let Q be actual pages and K newly needed character-reference generation calls. Art calls change from P to **Q+K** on a cold run and to **Q** with qualified references already available. Default P=8 currently occupies Q=2 pages: 8→2 is 75% fewer story-art calls; with K=2 it is 8→4, 50% fewer initial image calls. Larger page canvases, quality rejection, warm-up, and reference acquisition can erase cost/time savings, so qualify on paid receipts and wall time—not this arithmetic. Reserve reference and page recovery separately; do not preserve an unrelated 2P worker ceiling by habit.

## Acceptance comparisons that prove the real behavior

### Isolated, zero-spend contracts

- Test page groups for 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, and 12 ordered panels, including partial last pages and nonuniform layouts. Each approved beat maps exactly once; duplicate, absent, swapped, or out-of-bounds regions fail before spend.
- Test identity input byte propagation through the **actual adapter**: the reference hashes, image bytes/keys, character IDs, provider/model revision, geometry, and seed must reach the real request shape. Same input reproduces the cache key; changing a reference's bytes under the same asset ID invalidates it. Changing only lettering layout must not repurchase page art.
- Test exact cold/warm/repair call counts: Q+K, Q, zero on fully accepted resume, one failed page only on targeted recovery. Inject failure immediately after paid response, while writing the receipt, and while downloading durable bytes. Unknown paid outcomes must not become retries.
- Negative tests: a ten-panel plan missing panel 9; incorrect page order; duplicated face; wrong clothing; missing hand draw for opening or last panel; crop masking the key prop; shortened narration; one unreadable bubble. Distinguish deterministic rejection from visual findings rather than inventing a pass for unmeasured appearance.

### Integrated actual block, with only external boundaries controlled

- Run the real compiler/automatic route selection, approved-story binding, module budget reservation, comic block, page coordinator, image adapter serialization, renderer inputs, QA inputs, and storage mapping. Capture the calls reaching external transports. Confirm no legacy per-panel generation branch is also invoked.
- Assert provider page receipts bind exactly to the stored final page bytes and the renderer consumes those same bytes and reviewed regions. Ensure required story coverage and final narration duration survive all the way to the final-master/release gate.
- Retry after a simulated assembly failure and verify accepted reference/page/audio outputs are reused with zero paid resubmission. Exercise page-only and lettering-only repairs independently.
- Preserve ordinary per-panel route behavior until the new capability actually qualifies; do not silently route an existing locked channel to an unproven candidate. The unfamiliar-channel test must select and configure the qualified page capability itself, not receive a manually authored final story/page from the agent.

### Real provider and full native-render comparison

Start with one frozen eight-panel story spanning two pages, at least two distinguishable recurring characters, a new pose, an interaction, a small causal prop, and late-story identity/wardrobe continuity. Reuse the accepted narration and script for A/B fairness; independently generate the baseline panel art and candidate full-page art. Preserve untouched prompts/responses, input references, provider receipts, exact settings, bytes, final master, and review findings. No hand-written replacement art or hidden prompt correction.

Sample **every** panel at draw onset, mid-draw, completion, and readable hold, plus opening, every page turn, last frame, and every reported-risk window. Inspect final-framed output rather than only raw source pages. Review blinded A/B for story readability, linework/face detail, identity, action/prop accuracy, composition, lettering, and continuity. Require zero missing beats, zero undrawn required regions, zero blocking defects, and no worse visual quality/identity than the accepted baseline. Record time-to-first-story, revealed-content/hand coverage, unwanted blank/static time, and narration drift separately. A faster reveal must not outrun the spoken cue or remove the readable hold.

Measure cold and resumed provider calls, actual billed cost, worker warm-up, image generation, review, local reveal/encode, complete wall time, and regeneration fraction. Require real positive total-cost and time gains, not only fewer nominal calls. If the first page/canvas configuration loses quality, keep it unqualified and investigate resolution/layout/model capability; do not lower the quality threshold. After the bounded pilot passes, qualify a substantially different automatic comic channel and the whiteboard-specific 2×2 board experiment separately. One comic win does not qualify whiteboard cropping, native labels, or every art style.
